The problem

Your organization already has the answers. They’re just scattered.

Four systems. Four partial stories.Every tool you run holds a piece of how work happens. None hold the whole thing, so leaders decide on instinct and lagging reports.

One model. The whole picture.Teemly connects the pieces into a shared organizational context, so every decision starts from how work really happens.

People

HR knows who is on each team, not how the week is spent.

Work

Project tools know what was planned, not what pushed it out.

Technology

Finance knows what software costs, not which tools earn it.

AI

You rolled out AI. Its impact on work is still a guess.

How Teemly works

Connect. Understand. Improve.

Step 1

Connect

Deploy the lightweight Teemly agent and connect the systems you already use.

  • Map teams, roles and reporting lines
  • Choose what’s collected, by policy
  • Tell employees what’s measured and why
Step 2

Understand

Teemly builds your organizational model and sets a baseline for how work happens today.

  • Work rhythm and focus patterns
  • Tool adoption and seat usage
  • Workload and wellbeing signals
Step 3

Improve

Act on specific recommendations and measure the change against your baseline.

  • Recommendations with a clear owner
  • Before-and-after tracking
  • Reports ready for leadership reviews
